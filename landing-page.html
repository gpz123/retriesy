FAQ

Common questions

How is this different from Stripe's built-in Smart Retries?
Stripe Smart Retries uses generic ML — it doesn't know your specific customer patterns. Retriesy analyzes your own retry success/failure history to optimize timing, plus adds smart dunning emails and a frictionless card update page. Our users typically see 20-30% higher recovery rates vs. Stripe defaults.
Is $29/mo worth it if I only make $1,000 MRR?
Yes. With $1,000 MRR and an 8% failure rate, you lose ~$80/month to failed payments. Stripe's default recovers ~$30. Retriesy recovers ~$60. That's $30 extra per month — Retriesy pays for itself in the first week.
Do my customers see anything different?
Only if they need to. They'll get friendly emails (from your brand, not Stripe) if their payment fails. The card update page is white-labeled to match your product. Most customers who update their card successfully complete the payment — they weren't leaving, they just had a temporary card issue.
How long does setup take?
About 10 minutes. Connect your Stripe account via OAuth, configure your retry rules (or use our smart defaults), and you're live. No code changes required. Your first retry will fire within hours.
What happens to my data?
We store only webhook event data (payment IDs, failure codes, retry attempts) — never your customers' card details. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We're fully Stripe partner-compliant.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly plans cancel anytime, no questions asked. Your retry rules and data are preserved for 30 days if you decide to come back.
